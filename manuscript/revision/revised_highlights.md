# Highlights

- All 10 532 CLASH tests are audited. The models use 10 389 tests (occurrence) and 9 333 tests (discharge), with every RF and CF category kept.
- RF = 4 tests have 2.3 times the residual variance of RF = 1 tests, consistently across learners and validation schemes. RF 1–3 show no ordered trend.
- The complexity factor shows no ordered relation to geometry-level error. CF = 3 is the difficult class, and much of its error comes from a few series.
- Excluding category-4 tests changes the other predictions by at most 0.007 (fixed learner) or 0.014 (re-tuned, not significant) but degrades category-4 predictions by about 0.25 log10 units.
- Quality-aware conformal intervals with all tests retained raise RF = 4 coverage from 77 % to 89 %. CF = 3 remains under-covered.
