Generated by `revision/build_final_report.py` from `revision/tables` and the original tables. Every value can be traced to a table; the audit files are in `revision/audit`.

Alternative partitions and the deep-water substitution are reported only as sensitivity analyses.

## C. RF and CF validity

* RF = 4: residual-variance ratio vs RF = 1 = 2.34 [1.41, 3.81]; 2.13–3.14 over all learners and validation schemes, every interval > 1; 2.34–2.84 over geometry definitions; 3.66 when 100 RF = 4 tests with missing toe conditions are added by deep-water substitution (the complete-case value is likely conservative). **Demonstrated (as predictive association).**
* Specificity: RF4/RF2 1.70 [1.02, 2.83]; RF4/RF3 1.56 [0.93, 2.71]; no trend among RF 1–3. RF is a flag, not an ordered scale.
* Repeatability (near-replicate repeat SD of RF = 4 pairs): 0.5 %: 0.21, 1 %: 0.22, 2 %: 0.28, 5 %: 0.39; inconclusive, tolerance-dependent, 66 pairs at 1 %. Replicate / within-geometry variance 56 % [31 %, 93 %]. **Measurement error not identifiable.**
* CF: no ordered relation to geometry-level error (min. detectable ratio for CF = 4: 3.0). CF = 3 ratio 2.18, 1.46 [0.77, 2.74] without series 506. CF varies within 32 geometries, mostly with wave conditions. **CF is not validated as a geometry-level difficulty measure; absence of a trend is not proof of no effect.**
* Hierarchy: series 13 %, geometry 29 %, within 58 %; within share 47 %–58 % across geometry definitions. Laboratory effects not identifiable.

## F. Engineering implications

* EurOtop (mean-value formulae, valid domain, 4083 tests): RMSE 0.969 vs 0.526 (XGBoost, held-out geometries); not independent (EurOtop partly calibrated on CLASH).
* Allowable discharge 1 l/s/m at Hm0 = 2 m: missed exceedances among RF = 4 exceedances 7.8 % (retain all) → 18.3 % (exclude RF = 4); decision changed for 17.1 % of RF = 4 vs 4.0 % of common tests.
* Excluding category 4 removes 99 % of depth-limited tests, 31 % of low-freeboard tests and all chute structures.
* Data-quality policies therefore materially change overtopping decisions for exactly the conditions the excluded tests represent; thresholds are a Froude-scaled sensitivity grid, not design criteria.

## I. Remaining limitations

* No independent laboratory reports, controlled repeats or expert re-rating: correctness of individual ratings and pure measurement error are not identifiable.
* No structure or laboratory identifiers: geometries are reconstructed; laboratory effects are not identifiable.
* RF = 4 tests with emergent toes lack a toe wave height; the regression describes complete-case RF = 4 tests only.
* CF = 4 rests on 7 series; RF = 4 repeatability on 66 pairs (limited power).
* CF = 3 under-coverage persists; no scale available prospectively reproduces the RF benefit.
* Allowable discharges are a sensitivity grid; EurOtop comparison not independent.
* Conformal hyper-parameters were tuned on partitions including calibration geometries (mean and scale models were not).
* Paper 1 / Paper 2 overlap only conceptually checked; several references and the CLASH WP2 report must be verified by the authors.
* Times New Roman is not installed in the build environment; figures use the metrically identical Liberation Serif.
